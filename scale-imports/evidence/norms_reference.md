## Methodology

Thresholds are derived from published normative data:
- **3-tier:** M-1SD (low), M (moderate), M+1SD (high)
- **4-tier:** M-1SD (low), M-0.5SD (moderate-low), M+0.5SD (moderate-high), M+1SD (high)
- **Clinical scales:** Use published clinical cutoffs when available

## 2026-06-21 评分修复记录

### PSS-10 Q6 修复
- **问题**: Q6"发现自己无法应对所有必须做的事情"被错误标为反向计分
- **证据**: Cohen et al. (1983) "A Global Measure of Perceived Stress", Journal of Health and Social Behavior, 24(4), 385-396. PSS-10标准反向题: Q4,Q5,Q7,Q8（共4题），Q6为正向计分
- **修复**: Q6 改为正向计分 (从不=0→总是=4)

### BIS-11 子量表修复
- **问题**: 子量表分配为7+7+16，标准应为8+11+11；反向题14个，标准应为11个
- **证据**: Patton JH, Stanford MS, Barratt ES (1995) "Factor Structure of the Barratt Impulsiveness Scale", Journal of Clinical Psychology, 51(6), 761-767. Stanford et al. (2009) Psychiatry Research, 168(1), 35-42.
- **标准子量表**: Attention={1,2,3,4,5,6,11,28}, Motor={7,8,9,10,16,17,19,21,22,25,26}, Nonplanning={12,13,14,15,18,20,23,24,27,29,30}
- **标准反向题**: {1,7,8,9,10,12,13,15,20,29,30}（11题）
- **修复**: 重新分配子量表 + 修正反向题集

### LOT-R 题目映射修复
- **问题**: Q1(乐观)被错误排除，Q8(非标准)被错误计入悲观维度，Q9(非标准)被错误计入乐观维度
- **证据**: Scheier MF, Carver CS, Bridges MW (1994) "Distinguishing Optimism from Neuroticism", Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 67(6), 1063-1078. Carver CS官方评分指南: www.psy.miami.edu/faculty/ccarver/lot-r.html
- **标准**: 乐观={1,4,10}(直接), 悲观={3,7,9}(反向), 填充={2,5,6,8}(不计分)
- **中文映射**: Q1→乐观, Q3→乐观, Q7→乐观, Q4→悲观(反向), Q5→悲观(反向), Q6→悲观(反向), Q2/Q8/Q9/Q10→填充
- **修复**: Q1从填充→乐观, Q8从悲观→排除, Q9从乐观→排除
